# Current Figure 4(a--f): GW100

The current manuscript uses aug-SZV-MOLOPT-GTH-tier-2 RI-RS results at 0.5, 3, and 5 Å with `EPS_FILTER=1e-12`. Their raw files are under each `Figure_4[a-f]/current_eps12/ri<RI>_rs<RS>/<molecule>/`. The six paired panels share three sets of calculations. `molecule_errors.csv` lists every normal run, its original Noctua path, repository-relative path, SHA-256 hashes, frontier energies and signed errors against the TensorGW reference. The reference outputs are in `Figure_4a/reproducibility/tensor_references/GW100_TensorGW/`, with four updated references in `Figure_4a/reproducibility/selected_references/`.

The `input.inp` files are adapted to the public CP2K `master` schema. Their original SHA-256 values remain in `historical_input_sha256` and in `Figure_1a/reproducibility/public_master_input_migration_20261017.csv`; the original contents are available at Git commit `768b14d59d5870e92a01ca9bcd544a9e3a9af84f`. The archived outputs were obtained with those original inputs, not with the adapted files.

All 10,800 molecule/grid combinations completed normally and appear in the 216 manuscript means; every point covers 100 molecules. `missing.csv` is empty. Four LiH results at 0.5 Å and RS/AO=2 have a negative quasiparticle gap and are documented as grid diagnostics. `aggregate.csv` gives pointwise mean errors, `plot_coordinates.csv` records the manuscript values, and `summary.json` gives coverage. `python3 Figure_4a/reproducibility/current_gw100/reproduce.py` checks raw hashes, CP2K end markers, input filters, orbital edges, reference hashes, all aggregate values and all plotted coordinates. The submission manifest and Slurm resource accounting are in `retry_manifest_20261012.json` and `retry_resources_20261012.json`.
